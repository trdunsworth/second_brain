# Wisdom Sources Map of Content

This MOC connects philosophical, psychological, and spiritual traditions that inform personal development and the [[Attitude Adjustment Project]].

## Philosophical Traditions

### [[Stoicism_Research_Document]]
**Core:** Dichotomy of control, virtue ethics, emotional regulation
**Key Figures:** Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Epictetus
**Applications:** Resilience, decision-making, stress reduction
**Related:** [[David_Burns_CBT_Research_Document]] (cognitive techniques)

### [[Taoism_Research_Document]]
**Core:** Wu wei (non-action), harmony with nature, simplicity
**Key Figures:** Lao Tzu, Chuang Tzu
**Applications:** Letting go, flow states, natural living

### [[Druidry_Research_Document]]
**Core:** Nature connection, seasonal awareness, spiritual practice
**Tradition:** Celtic spiritual path
**Applications:** Environmental awareness, ritual, mindfulness

## Buddhist Traditions

### [[Buddhism_Research_Document]]
**Core:** Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path, dependent origination
**Tradition:** General Buddhist philosophy
**Applications:** Suffering reduction, mindfulness, ethical living

### [[Pema_Chodron_Research_Document]]
**Core:** Groundlessness, compassion, shenpa (trigger)
**Key Works:** When Things Fall Apart, Start Where You Are
**Applications:** Working with difficulty, emotional resilience

### [[Chogyam_Trungpa_Research_Document]]
**Core:** Spiritual materialism, warrior tradition, emptiness
**Key Works:** Cutting Through Spiritual Materialism
**Applications:** Authentic practice, ego awareness

### [[Surya_Das_Research_Document]]
**Core:** Tibetan Buddhist practice, meditation, awakening
**Key Works:** Awakening the Buddha Within
**Applications:** Meditation practice, spiritual development

### [[Akimitsu_Takeuchi_Research_Document]]
**Core:** Japanese Buddhist perspective
**Tradition:** Eastern Buddhist philosophy
**Applications:** Contemplative practice

## Psychological Traditions

### [[David_Burns_CBT_Research_Document]]
**Core:** Cognitive behavioral therapy, cognitive distortions, mood journaling
**Key Works:** Feeling Good, The Feeling Good Handbook
**Applications:** Depression management, cognitive restructuring, [[daily_mood_log_template]]
**Related:** [[Attitude Adjustment Project]], [[daily_reflection_template]]

### [[Dr_Jonice_Webb_Research_Document]]
**Core:** Childhood emotional neglect (CEN)
**Key Works:** Running on Empty
**Applications:** Understanding emotional gaps, self-compassion

### [[Dr_Brené_Brown_Research_Document]]
**Core:** Vulnerability, shame resilience, wholehearted living
**Key Works:** Daring Greatly, The Gifts of Imperfection
**Applications:** Authenticity, courage, connection
