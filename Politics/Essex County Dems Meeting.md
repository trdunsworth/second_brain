### Election Day

We need poll greeters, poll observers, and deputies at each precinct. We can have phones present, but off unless needing to call out. No pictures.

#### Poll Observers

The DPVA allows us to select our own personnel, providing they are properly trained. Previously, the party selected where the observers were sent. We are self-organizing and will be able to assign ourselves. We do have to communicate who will be at what precinct and how long you'll be there. The form needs to be given to the precinct captain. They retain it while you are working. They are to sign it and return it at the end of your shift.

For observers, you observe and if you see anything, report it to Cyanne and she will handle it. Her example is if a husband tells his wife how to vote. We only call the boiler room if it's egregious. In those cases, we notify Cyanne or Angela and they contact the party boiler room. 

Each precinct has two chiefs, one for each major party. 

The manual and training links were sent by Cyanne via email. She does not expect people to stay the entire day, but would like 1 to 2 hour shifts. Last election was around 2700 voters cast ballots, currently we have 1800+ votes already cast.

Cyanne believes we need more observers than greeters due to the threats of Federal intervention. 

You can't wear anything political. Please keep your weapons at home for this. That is now a Virginia statute. Essex Co SO will have deputies present at each precinct.

### Reverend Hundley's Presentation


Currently on the county board of elections. He has created a training program for poll workers. Multiple oaths sworn by the poll workers. The ballots are returned to the courts who then retain them until disposed of. The ECSO presence is usually made of SROs. 

An observer must be able to hear the name, but nothing else by statute.

Provisional ballots are not run through the scanner. Those are manually counted after state verification. We do have same day registration (responsibility of the chief) and that person can vote by provisional ballot. 

A person who votes by provisional ballot can attend the canvas to defend their ballot/vote. Final canvases are done 5 days after.
