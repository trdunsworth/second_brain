## Starting Notes

It's Monday again. Spent a lot of the weekend harvesting a lot of resources for old TTRPGs. Outside of D&D because I want to play it again, and Pathfinder because it was a good successor and could use more of the material for D&D v3.5, I am trying to ascertain why I was worried about the rest. 

I enjoyed WoD when I lived in LA, but I saw the failures in it. Maybe I just enjoy the overarching setting enough to have fun with it. Maybe I see a way to correct its failures. I went through the Wikipedia articles for the system and it seems like I wasn't the only one who had those issues. It could be interesting to see where it leads. The challenge for me was how to balance out the different games to see where they led. It wasn't like Shadowrun or D&D where you could come up with different scenarios that could link together to create a campaign.

Maybe it was a lack of my own imagination, but I can always fix that.
