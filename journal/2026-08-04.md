## Starting Notes

I had a very interesting dream overnight. I'd gone into a huge university library. In the back of my mind, I kept thinking that it was KU, but it didn't look like Watson at all. The library was huge and I immediately went to my left to a non-fiction section. I picked up a book on chess and carried it with me. I wandered through the stacks looking for something else. I knew that I needed some paper for notes, but didn't have any. I went back out of the stacks, which seemed like a bunch of rooms connected together with tables in the middle of each, and went looking through my memories for where I could find some. I went past the main desk which looked more like it belonged in a luxury hotel. I went up a small set of stairs to a lounge and reading area back to the cafeteria. It was bigger than I remembered, but the layout was still similar to previous dreams. The thought entered my mind that it had only been built about 9 years earlier. There was an old exit door for a mini student union, but the space was empty and closed. I went to a couple of other places. One had old discarded notebooks in front, but I felt uncomfortable taking one of them. I was looking for one of my favourite harvest places where an old line printer used to live. They still have left out the old printer paper for students to use. I couldn't find it and I wanted to get to the old stacks where the space was much larger and the most recent books were from the 80's to find some other books to consult. I can remember what the stacks looked like from a previous dream, but I never got there.
