# Thursday, August 27, 2026

## Starting Notes

There's a lot to unpack some days. I had an interesting dream overnight about preparing to leave to go somewhere, making certain that the space I was leaving was neat and tidy and that everything was ready for either the next occupant or me if I ever needed to return. This morning, heading to the shower, I noticed the scar on my left wrist, thought about a Toastmasters' speech, titling it, *"A Bad Day at 14"* and then how everyone is carrying something, how we're talking about it in our industry more and more, not only to protect the health of the people we serve, but to protect the health of the people we serve with. I joined the 911-988 interchange group because of that. It wasn't just about a child with a profound mental health diagnosis, nor about a teacher in his high school whose mental health challenges weren't properly met; leading to his death. It was also about that one bad day at 14, surviving spousal abuse, surviving other stresses that I kept silent because I thought everyone had it worse. I had access to help and I accepted that help throughout my life because my status as a cis-gendered heterosexual white male made all of those resources readily available. I don't discuss it as openly as I could because I still have that societal stigma baked in. I want to ensure that is available to everyone. I shouldn't get better access because of how I appear. I'm no more special than anyone else. 

That's when I suddenly started thinking about the ideas for a *"Bad Ass"* church. I was travelling home from a trip when I passed a church I've passed hundreds of times, Broadus Church and misread the sign because I'm dyslexic and I was tired. That's the right potent mix. So I've been thinking, what would make a *"Bad Ass"* church? First I would add by subtraction. Let's get rid of the building and leadership cadre. If G_d is truly omnipresent, like I heard growing up, then we don't need a building, we need a community. The building is that same silo we're lamenting in industry. We're separating out from other people. I know we need people to organize what the church does, but it doesn't need to be so strictly formal. I find myself thinking of Friends leaders who facilitate letting the Spirit guide the meeting. Maybe we have a facilitator who guides the meetings. I think that the group should make a habit of meeting places where they can do the most good for the community, such as a stranger's house who needs help, a homeless shelter, or a battered spouse shelter. Go there, work, listen to a message, and then eat together. It's not about the building or promoting yourselves, it's about being present, showing G_d's omnipotence by being that power, and showing G_d's love through loving others in service.
