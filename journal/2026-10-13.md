I learned a lot from Bob and I learned that I could do the job. I think that I wanted to take it to honour his legacy and his training. The arc of my education went a different way. When he leaves, I know that someone will replace him. It will not be me, and I shouldn't look to others to ask me if I want to move that direction, career-wise, so I can feel better about not applying for the position.
