## 📝 Speech Content

### Opening

After starting to hit the speaking circuit, discussing the use of data analytics in the 9-1-1 industry, I understood that I could not always use my center's data to illustrate various parts of the analytical process.
### Body
#### Main Point 1

The starting point was for tabular event data that could be analyzed. However, the first attempts created data that, while capable of being analyzed, was not realistic in distribution or appearance.
#### Main Point 2

In preparing for my dissertation, I started examining the need for a volume generator that could allow for forecasting without exposing a centers data. 
#### Main Point 3

In order to make the data useful to analysts and companies that develop AI-assisted products, greater realism needed to be added to the software which changed what the software could do and how it could be done. 
### Conclusion

SynthCCD, the first 9-1-1-centric synthetic data generator has been publicly released to benefit the industry overall.
