# Rook Endgames - The Most Common Practical Ending

> **Theme**: Rook Endgames  
> **Frequency**: 50%+ of all endgames  
> **Priority**: #2 - Master After K+P  
> **Aggression**: Universal (activity > material)  
> **Estimated Hours**: 12

---

## Golden Rule: **ACTIVITY > MATERIAL**

A rook on the 7th rank is often worth a pawn.  
Active king + active rook > passive king + extra pawn.

---

## The 4 Theoretical Pillars

### 1. Lucena Position (WINNING)
**Rook + Pawn vs Rook - Pawn on 7th, king in front**

```
White: Ke1, Pe7, Rf1
Black: Ke8, Rf8
```
**Winning Method - "Building the Bridge"**:
```
1. Rf4! (bridge)
2. Kf7 (king approaches)
3. Rf8 (rook behind pawn)
4. Ke7 (king protects pawn)
5. e8=Q (promotion)
```
**Key**: Rook on 4th rank = bridge. King MUST get in front.

**MEMORIZE THIS**. It's the #1 practical rook ending.

---

### 2. Philidor Position (DRAWING)
**Defender: Rook on 6th/3rd rank, king in front of pawn**

```
White: Ke6, Pe6, Rf6 (6th rank)
Black: Kh8, Rf1
```
**Drawing Method**:
1. Rook on 6th rank cuts off king
2. When pawn advances → Rook goes BEHIND pawn
3. Check from behind FOREVER: Rh1-h6-h1-h6...
4. King shelters behind pawn

**Rule**: Rook on 6th = draw. Rook behind = win (usually).

---

### 3. Vancura Position (DRAWING)
**Rook behind pawn on 2nd/7th rank**

```
White: Ke6, Pe5, Rf2
Black: Kh8, Rf1
```
**Drawing Method**:
1. Rook stays behind pawn (f2)
2. Checks from side: Rf2-f6-f2...
3. King shelters: Kf7/e7
4. If pawn advances → Rook checks from behind

**Works when**: Pawn not too far advanced, king not cut off.

---

### 4. Rook Behind Passed Pawn (ATTACKING IDEAL)
**Rook BEHIND passed pawn = MAXIMUM ACTIVITY**

```
White: Ke3, Pe4, Re1 (behind)
Black: Kd7, Rd8
```
**Winning Plan**:
1. Push pawn: e5, e6, e7
2. King supports: Kd4, Ke5, Kf6
3. Rook checks/supports from behind
4. Promotion inevitable

**Rule**: **Rook behind passer = Win** (if king helps).

## Practical Principles

### 1. Rook on 7th Rank
**Activity compensates for pawn deficit.**
- Attacks pawns
- Restricts king
- Creates mating threats
- **Don't trade it!** Keep the 7th rank.

### 2. King Activity = Life
**Centralize king EARLY.**
- Kd4/e4/d5/e5 in center
- Support passed pawns
- Attack enemy pawns
- Avoid checks by staying central

### 3. Cutting Off the King
**Rook on rank/file limits enemy king.**
- 2 ranks/files away = usually draw
- 1 rank/file away = often win
- **Count the distance!**

### 4. Checking Distance
**Perpetual check = Draw.**
- Need 3+ files/ranks for safety
- Rook checks from far away
- King cannot hide

### 5. Two Weaknesses Principle
**One weakness = defendable. Two = lost.**
- Create second target (kingside + queenside)
- King can't defend both
- Rook can't cover both

## Common Practical Scenarios

### Scenario A: Up a Pawn, Rook Ending
**Plan**: Trade rooks → K+P (if winning) OR keep rooks → activate
- **Lucena**: Know it cold
- **Active rook**: 7th rank, behind passer
- **King**: Centralize, support pawn
- **Don't rush**: Technique > speed

### Scenario B: Down a Pawn, Rook Ending
**Plan**: Philidor/Vancura → Draw
- Get rook on 6th/3rd rank
- King in front of pawn
- Perpetual check if needed
- **Stalemate tricks** with rook pawn

### Scenario C: Equal Pawns, Active Rook
**Plan**: Press for win
- Rook on 7th
- King active
- Create passed pawn
- Two weaknesses

### Scenario D: Rook vs 2 Pawns (or 2 Pawns vs Rook)
**Specific knowledge needed.**
- Connected pawns on 6th = draw vs rook
- Split pawns = rook wins
- King position critical
