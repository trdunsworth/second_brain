# Worldbuilding MOC

A map of the worldbuilding toolkit. Everything here is built from the **System Reference Document 5.2.1** (CC BY 4.0) — rules the SRD actually contains, page-cited — plus verified free external resources. Nothing here invents a setting for you: the toolkit gives you the rules hooks, the gaps, and the generator tables, and you supply the names.

> [!warning] Ground rules for this toolkit
> 1. **No invented canon.** Plane names, deity names, prices, DCs, and stat blocks are only stated if they appear in [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1]] (cited as `SRD p. N`) or in a resource listed in [[Free Resources Directory]].
> 2. **`[GAP]` means "verified absent."** The SRD has no cosmology chapter, no deity list, and no faction, kingdom, or calendar rules. Those are marked **[GAP]** and are yours to invent (or to source from the 2024 *Dungeon Master's Guide*, which is not in this vault).
> 3. **Generator tables are seeders, not canon.** Every d6/d20/d100 table in these notes is either (a) an SRD table republished with a page cite, or (b) a clearly marked *homebrew* table of prompts — never a table of facts.

## How to use it

1. **Scope first.** Read [[Worldbuilding Checklist]] and pick your tier: tier 1 threatens *farmsteads or villages*, tier 2 *cities and kingdoms*, tier 3 *whole regions*, tier 4 *the world or multiverse* (SRD pp. 23–24). Everything else scales from that decision.
2. **Build the frame.** [[Cosmology and Planes]] → [[Pantheon and Deities Template]] → [[Nations and Politics Template]]. In that order: the planes determine the gods, the gods determine the factions, the factions determine the borders.
3. **Fill the frame.** [[Settlements and Locations Template]] and [[NPC Framework]] turn the frame into places and people; [[History and Timelines]] gives them a past; [[Economy and Downtime]] gives them a price.
4. **Run it.** [[Encounter and Adventure Design]] converts the world into sessions using the SRD's own encounter math.
5. **When you hit a `[GAP]`,** decide deliberately: house rule, buy the DMG, or pull from an open-licensed alternative in [[Free Resources Directory]].

## Shared conventions

- `> [!src] SRD p. N — Section Name` carries the citation for the rule being described. Page numbers are the printed footers of [[SRD_CC_v5.2.1]] (each page ends with `N  System Reference Document 5.2.1`).
- **`[GAP]`** marks content verified absent from SRD 5.2.1 by full-text search — see [[Worldbuilding Checklist#SRD coverage map]] for the consolidated list.
- **`*homebrew*`** inside a table marks a prompt table authored here, not an SRD table.
- Rules-terms are capitalized as the SRD capitalizes them (Advantage, Influence action, Magic action) so you can grep the PDF later.
- Conversion from older editions is handled in [[3.5e-to-5.5e-Conversion]] — this toolkit does not repeat it.

## SRD coverage map (quick view)

| Worldbuilding need | SRD 5.2.1 status |
| --- | --- |
| Planes of existence | **Partial** — named and reachable (SRD pp. 209, 214–216), but no cosmology chapter **[GAP]** |
| Deities and domains | **Absent** — no deity list; only the Life Domain subclass exists (SRD p. 40) **[GAP]** |
| Factions, kingdoms, calendars, holidays | **Absent** — no rules text **[GAP]** |
| Settlement tiers | **Present implicitly** — village/town/city gate services (SRD pp. 102, 205, 207) |
| NPCs | **Present** — attitude, Influence, 26 humanoid stat blocks (SRD pp. 10, 177, 260–337) |
| Prices, lifestyles, crafting | **Present** (SRD pp. 89, 101–103, 206–207) |
| Encounter math | **Present** — XP Budget per Character, Low/Moderate/High (SRD p. 202); daily XP budget **[GAP]** |
| Travel, terrain, environment, traps | **Present** (SRD pp. 12, 192, 195–201) |
