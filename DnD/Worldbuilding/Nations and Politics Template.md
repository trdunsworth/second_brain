# Nations and Politics Template

The SRD has no kingdom rules, no demographics, and no war rules — but it does have *scope*, *law*, *language*, *history*, and *trade*, which is enough to build a political map that matters at the table.

> [!warning] The big [GAP]
> Searched and absent from SRD 5.2.1: `kingdom` (1 hit — flavor only), `province`, `empire`, `duchy`, `treaty`, `alliance`, `population`, `demographics`, `government`, `taxes` (as a rule), `court`. There are **no nation-creation rules, no government structures, no war/battle resolution, and no economy-of-states rules.** Design those yourself or lift an open-licensed system from [[Free Resources Directory]].

## What the SRD does constrain

| Hook | SRD cite | Substance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scope of threat by tier** | SRD pp. 23–24 | Tier 1: *local farmsteads or villages*. Tier 2: *"dangers that threaten cities and kingdoms."* Tier 3: *"threats to whole regions."* Tier 4: *"the fate of the world or even the order of the multiverse."* Your political map should not be bigger than the tier you chose |
| **History skill** | SRD p. 9 | Intelligence: *"Recall lore about historical events, people, **nations**, and cultures."* Politics you can't recall on a dice roll is invisible politics |
| **Alignment and law** | SRD pp. 21–22 | Lawful Neutral individuals *"act in accordance with law, tradition, or personal codes"*; the Chaotic Good example is literally political: *"A rebel who waylays a cruel baron's tax collectors and uses the stolen money to help the poor"* (p. 21). Alignment is your first political vocabulary |
| **An explicit law on the books** | SRD p. 197 | *"In some settings, laws prohibit the possession and use of poison, but an illicit dealer or unscrupulous…"* — the SRD's only statute. Make your jurisdiction's laws by extending this pattern |
| **Languages encode nations** | SRD p. 20 | Standard languages *"originated with the first members of the most prominent species in the setting"*; rare languages are *"secret or derived from other planes of existence."* Bilingual borders and secret-state languages are rules-legal |
| **Guildhalls and temples as infrastructure** | SRD p. 169 | *"Many major temples, guildhalls, and other important places have permanent teleportation circles"* — whoever controls the circles controls trade |
| **Trade routes are stat-blocked** | SRD pp. 100–101 | Ship speeds, crews, cargo in tons, and costs: Galley 4 mph / 150 tons / 30,000 GP; Sailing Ship 2 mph / 100 tons / 10,000 GP; Airship 8 mph / 1 ton / 40,000 GP; Warship 25,000 GP. Repairs: 20 GP per HP per day, halved in a city shipyard (p. 101) |
| **Political NPCs exist** | SRD pp. 302, 312, 296 | Knight (CR 3, 700 XP), Noble (CR 1/8, 25 XP), Guard Captain (CR 4, 1,100 XP) — plus Assassin (CR 8, p. 260) and Spy (CR 1, 200 XP, p. 329) for the parts of politics nobody prints |
| **Soldier and Sage backgrounds** | SRD p. 83 | Soldier (Athletics, Intimidation) and Sage (Arcana, History) are the two party-facing entries into state power |
| **Custom background** | SRD p. 192 | Build "Court Envoy," "Frontier Warden," or "Diplomat" under the official custom-background rules |
| **Trinket hooks** | SRD pp. 26–27 | *"The deed for a parcel of land in a realm unknown to you"* (p. 26); *"A receipt of deposit at a bank in a far-off city"* (p. 27); *"A whistle made from gold-colored wood"* (p. 27) |

## Running politics at the table

1. **Politics is a social encounter, not a cutscene.** Use the Influence action (SRD p. 10) with a stated goal: shift the official's attitude from Indifferent to Friendly (attitudes: SRD p. 177). Give them a want (SRD p. 11: *"learn an NPC's goals and then use that information"*).
2. **Let terrain do the arguing.** Border disputes follow the Travel Terrain table: a desert's 20/10/10 DCs versus an urban 20/15/15 (SRD p. 192) decide which province is actually governable.
3. **Make trade visible as numbers.** Cargo tons and speeds (SRD p. 101) plus the resale rule — equipment sells for half, trade goods retain full value (SRD p. 89) — is enough for a smuggling plot.
4. **Escalate NPCs with offices, not levels.** Noble (CR 1/8) → Guard Captain (CR 4) → Knight (CR 3) → Assassin (CR 8) is a natural ladder of who a nation can field (SRD pp. 312, 296, 302, 260).
5. **Write the recall.** If the party can't answer a History check about it (SRD p. 9), it isn't established yet.

## Confirmed [GAP]s in this area

1. **No government, law-code, taxation, or court rules.**
2. **No demographics, city-population, or kingdom-wealth tables.**
3. **No mass-combat, war, or naval-battle rules** (vehicle stats exist — SRD p. 101 — but no battle system).
4. **No calendar, season, holiday, or weather-climate rules** (weather appears only as spell effects, e.g. *Control Weather*).
5. **No renown/loyalty/influence-across-a-nation system.**
