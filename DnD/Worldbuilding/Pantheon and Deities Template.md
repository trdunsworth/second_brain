# Pantheon and Deities Template

Build a pantheon that the SRD 5.2.1 rules can actually *use* — and know exactly where the rules stop.

> [!warning] The big [GAP]
> **SRD 5.2.1 contains no deity list.** There is no pantheon chapter, no god stat block, no "gods of the setting" appendix. There is exactly **one** Cleric subclass — the **Life Domain** (SRD p. 40) — and no other domain mechanics (Knowledge, Light, Nature, Tempest, Trickery, War, and Death domains do **not** appear as playable rules). Everything below is either (a) a rule that exists, or (b) a blank you must fill in.

## What the SRD *does* give religion-wise

| Hook | SRD cite | Substance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Religion skill** | SRD p. 9 | Intelligence: *"Recall lore about gods, religious rituals, and holy symbols."* |
| **Life Domain (only domain)** | SRD p. 40 | *"The Life Domain focuses on the positive energy that helps sustain all life in the multiverse… particularly associated with agricultural deities, gods of healing or endurance, and gods of home and community."* Features: Life Domain Spells, Disciple of Life, Preserve Life, Blessed Healer, Supreme Healing |
| **Channel Divinity** | SRD pp. 37, 54 | Clerics and Paladins *"channel divine energy directly from the Outer Planes"* — gods and cosmology are mechanically linked |
| **Divine Intervention** | SRD p. 38 | Level 10 Cleric: *"call on your deity or pantheon to intervene"* — cast any Cleric spell of level 5 or lower without a slot |
| **Turn Undead** | SRD p. 37 | A divine weapon that presupposes an undead-haunted world |
| **Holy Symbols** | SRD pp. 95, 97 | Price list says *Holy Symbol: Varies* (p. 95); the detailed table gives **Amulet 5 GP, Emblem 5 GP, Reliquary 5 GP** (p. 97) — *"a Holy Symbol takes one of the forms in the Holy Symbols table"* (p. 97) |
| **Two named deities** | SRD pp. 218, 227 | *Ioun Stone*: *"named after Ioun, a god of knowledge and prophecy revered on some worlds."* Dragon Orb: one possible purpose is *"to advance the worship of Tiamat."* |
| **Planar Ally** | SRD p. 152 | *"You beseech an otherworldly entity for aid. The being must be known to you: a god, a demon prince, or some other being of cosmic power"* — sends a Celestial, Elemental, or Fiend |
| **Warlock patron** | SRD p. 76 | The Fiend Patron: *"Make a Deal with the Lower Planes… bargain with a demon lord, an archdevil, or another fiend"* |
| **Oath of Devotion** | SRD p. 56 | Paladins devoted to *"gods of law and good"* (also *"hold angels as the measure of personal devotion"*) |
| **Species origins** | SRD p. 83 | *"Some species can trace their origin to a single world, plane of existence, or god"* — species-as-divine-creation is legal |
| **Divine relics and foci** | SRD pp. 93, 97 | Reliquary (2 lb, 5 GP), Emblem (5 GP), Amulet (5 GP); *Holy Water* 25 GP (p. 97) |

> [!tip] The practical rule
> Because only the Life Domain has mechanics, **any deity you invent can grant any Cleric feature only if you (or the GM) re-skin or homebrew.** The clean approach: let your gods map onto the SRD's actual domain (Life), the SRD's warlock patron (Fiend), the SRD's paladin oath (Oath of Devotion), and *Narrative* divine power for everything else.

## Working the rules into worship

1. **Divine spellcasting is a service industry.** A settlement's tier decides whether 1st-level (village/town/city), 3rd–5th (town/city), or 9th-level (city) casting is even obtainable (SRD p. 102). Your pantheon's wealth and influence should track that table.
2. **Holy symbols are cheap; faith is not.** 5 GP for an Amulet, Emblem, or Reliquary (SRD p. 97) — so the *symbol* never limits a cleric; the *institution* does.
3. **The Outer Planes are the power grid.** Channel Divinity explicitly draws from them (SRD pp. 37, 54), so a deity's home plane is a mechanical, not just narrative, choice → [[Cosmology and Planes]].
4. **Ritual and taboo are yours.** The SRD gives one explicit law-flavoured hook (poison possession, SRD p. 197) and the Narrative Curses framework (SRD p. 193: broken vows, defiled tombs, murdered innocents → GM-designed curse effects). Build your religion's taboos on that pattern.

## Confirmed [GAP]s in this area

1. **No deity list, no divine domains beyond Life, no god statistics.** The [official SRD FAQ](https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document) confirms 5.2 removed *Appendix PH-B: Fantasy-Historical Pantheons* — so this gap is intentional. The free SRD 5.1 copy is linked in [[Free Resources Directory#Filling gaps from free, older, or open sources]].
2. **No rules for divine spell preparation by deity**, faith points, or divine boons outside the Life Domain.
3. **No holy-day, calendar, tithe, or church-hierarchy rules.**
4. **No Cleric alignment requirement** (SRD has no such rule — alignment is a free choice, SRD pp. 21–22).
5. **No afterlife mechanics** beyond "the spirit… departed for the Outer Planes" (SRD p. 180) and the *Revivify*-family spells on the Life Domain list (SRD p. 40).
