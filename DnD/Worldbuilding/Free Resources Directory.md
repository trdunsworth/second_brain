# Free Resources Directory

Everything else that is legal, free, and actually useful for this toolkit — where to read the rules, where to pull open-licensed material when you hit a `[GAP]`, and what each link is allowed to become in a product of yours. **Every URL below returned HTTP 200 from a normal browser request on 2026-09-27**; links known to be dead are quarantined in [[Free Resources Directory#Dead or redirected links]] instead of deleted, so you don't rediscover them the hard way.

> [!warning] Three rules for this list
> 1. **"Free to read" ≠ "free to republish."** SRD 5.2.1 text is CC BY 4.0; SRD 5.1 text is OGL 1.0a *and* CC BY 4.0; third-party content carries its own license (ORC, CC, MIT, OGL). Check per resource — see [[Free Resources Directory#Copyright care]].
> 2. **SRD content and third-party content live side by side on some sites** (notably the Open Gaming Network sites). On those, an entry with a **publisher** named is *somebody else's* material, not SRD.
> 3. **Anything flagged ⚠ is reference material, not copy-paste material.**

> [!tip] Version discipline
> Cite **5.2.1** or cite **5.1** — never blend them silently. The official FAQ records that SRD 5.1 is available under **both** OGL 1.0a and CC BY 4.0, while *newer* SRDs are released **under CC only**. Mixing an OGL 1.0a notice into a CC BY 4.0 5.2.1 product is the classic licensing error; see [[Free Resources Directory#Copyright care]].

## What SRD 5.2.1 changed (why old links and old notes break)

From the official FAQ and [[converting-to-srd-5.2.1]] — these are the deltas that matter to worldbuilding:

| Change in 5.2.1 | Consequence for you | Where it lands in this toolkit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **"Between Adventures" section removed** | No downtime activity chapter at all | [[Economy and Downtime]] `[GAP]`s |
| **Appendix PH-B (Fantasy-Historical Pantheons) removed** | No deity list, no suggested domains, no symbols | [[Pantheon and Deities Template]] — free 5.1 copy below |
| **The Planes of Existence appendix removed** | Planes are named in spells and items, but not described | [[Cosmology and Planes]] `[GAP]`s |
| **Deck of Many Things → *Mysterious Deck*** (SRD p. 231) | Old links say the retired name; search for *Mysterious Deck* | [[History and Timelines]] |
| **Orb of Dragonkind → *Dragon Orb*** (SRD p. 218) | Same — the rename avoids a protected trademark | [[Cosmology and Planes]] |
| **New Humanoid blocks added** | Guard Captain, Pirate, Pirate Captain, Tough Boss, Vampire Familiar (plus Goblin Boss, Bugbear Stalker, Hobgoblin Captain) | [[NPC Framework#SRD humanoid roster]] — **26** blocks, SRD pp. 260–337 |
| **Artificer, Aasimar, Beholder excluded; setting names dropped** | No Artificer class; **0 hits** in 5.2.1 for `Strahd` or `Orcus` (though *Tiamat* survives in the *Dragon Orb* text, SRD p. 218) | [[Worldbuilding Checklist#SRD coverage map]] |

> Treat generator output the way this toolkit treats its own `*homebrew*` tables: a **seeder of names and problems**, never canon. You still decide what's true, and every table you publish gets labeled as homebrew.

## Excluded or deliberately not listed

| Excluded | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Any site hosting SRD text **without the CC BY 4.0 attribution** | Fails the license the SRD itself imposes (SRD p. 1) |
| Pirated rulebook PDFs and scanned books | Not free-to-use in any sense |
| Paid marketplaces and subscriptions (DNDBeyond store, DMsGuild, Roll20 marketplace) | Out of scope — this list is *free* resources; DMsGuild/DriveThru are worth knowing as **sales channels** (see the Roll20 SRD page's guidance) |
| Tools or shops whose licensing isn't stated (dice shops, "free rules" mirrors of unclear origin) | Reference-only until the license is visible |
| Generators that output **published setting names** (Forgotten Realms deities, named adventures) | Your product inherits the trademark problem those names create |

## Copyright care

> [!license] The statement you must include
> This work includes material from the **System Reference Document 5.2.1 ("SRD 5.2.1")** by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd. The SRD 5.2.1 is licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License**, available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.
>
> — quoted verbatim from SRD p. 1, which requires it in "any work that uses" the SRD.

### SRD 5.2.1 (CC BY 4.0)

- ✅ Include the attribution statement above, unchanged.
- ✅ State that your work is **"compatible with fifth edition"** or **"5E compatible"** if you want a compatibility claim.
- ❌ Include **no other attribution** to Wizards of the Coast or its affiliates (the SRD says so directly, SRD p. 1).
- ❌ Do not imply endorsement: say the work is **unofficial and not endorsed by Wizards of the Coast**.
- ✅ CC BY 4.0 also lets you **adapt, remix, and sell** the material, as long as attribution survives and you indicate changes.

### SRD 5.1 (if you pull from the older free appendix)

Per the official FAQ, SRD 5.1 is dual-licensed **OGL 1.0a and CC BY 4.0** — you may choose either route:

| Route | What you owe |
| --- | --- |
| **CC BY 4.0** (recommended) | Attribution, indication of changes — same discipline as 5.2.1 |
| **OGL 1.0a** | Include the OGL license text and the **Section 15 copyright notice** listing every source you used (this is the notice printed at the end of the 5.1 SRD; [5esrd.com/legal-information](https://www.5esrd.com/legal-information/) shows the pattern) |

> [!warning] The one real trap
> Pantheon material from Appendix PH-B names **domains** (Knowledge, Light, Nature, Tempest, Trickery, War, Death) whose **mechanics are not in SRD 5.2.1** — 5.2.1 ships only the Life Domain (SRD p. 40). If you publish, say plainly which SRD each rule came from: *rules from SRD 5.2.1, deity flavor from SRD 5.1.*

### Third-party licenses on this list

| License | Seen on | Minimum obligation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CC BY 4.0** | SRD 5.2.1, 5e-srd-markdown, parts of Open5e | Attribution + indicate changes |
| **ORC** | Black Flag Roleplaying | Follow the licensed text; attribution as it specifies |
| **OGL 1.0a** | SRD 5.1, OGN sites, donjon notes | Required notices; **never** copy product identity |
| **MIT** | dnd5eapi, Kobold Plus code | Keep the copyright and permission notice |

### Republishing checklist

- [ ] CC BY 4.0 attribution statement present **verbatim** (SRD p. 1)
- [ ] No other Wizards attribution anywhere in the work
- [ ] "5E compatible" / "compatible with fifth edition" if a compatibility claim is made
- [ ] "Unofficial and not endorsed by Wizards of the Coast" stated
- [ ] Every generator table labeled as *homebrew*, not presented as SRD content
- [ ] SRD 5.1 material tagged with its origin (it is **not** in the 5.2.1 PDF)
- [ ] Third-party content checked against **its** license (ORC / CC / OGL / MIT)
- [ ] No excluded names, trademarks, or product identity (Artificer, Aasimar, Beholder, Strahd, Orcus are outside SRD 5.2.1)
- [ ] Page citations (`SRD p. N`) point at 5.2.1 printed footers, not 5.1 page numbers
