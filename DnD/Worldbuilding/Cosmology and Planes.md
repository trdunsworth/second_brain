# Cosmology and Planes

What SRD 5.2.1 actually tells you about the shape of the multiverse, what it refuses to tell you, and generator tables for filling the silence.

> [!warning] The big [GAP]
> The SRD 5.2.1 has **no cosmology chapter**. There is no "Planes of Existence" section, no diagram, no explanation of how the Material Plane relates to the Astral, the Feywild, or the Outer Planes. Everything below is assembled from incidental mentions in spells, magic items, monster traits, and skill descriptions. That is a feature for worldbuilders: *the plane taxonomy is yours to invent*. See [[Free Resources Directory]] for open-licensed cosmologies you can lift instead.

## What the SRD names

| Plane or category | Where it appears | What the SRD says (paraphrased) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Material Plane** | SRD pp. 20, 169, 216, 287, 311, 314 | The default plane; *Teleportation Circle* sigils are "on the Material Plane" (p. 169); one face of the *Cubic Gate* is keyed to it (p. 216); ethereal-sensing creatures perceive "60 feet into the Ethereal Plane when it is on the Material Plane" (p. 287) |
| **Feywild** | SRD pp. 118, 124, 133, 209, 254 | Home of conjured fey spirits (p. 118); *Banishment* sends displaced Fey there (p. 124); *Forbiddance* proofs an area against entry "by way of the Astral Plane, the Ethereal Plane, the Feywild, or the *Plane Shift* spell" (p. 133); Fey "are creatures tied to the Feywild or the forces of nature" (p. 254) |
| **Shadowfell** | SRD pp. 124, 133, 209 | *Banishment* sends displaced Undead there (p. 124); excluded by *Forbiddance* (p. 133); listed as a random inner-plane destination (p. 209) |
| **Astral Plane** | SRD pp. 111, 133, 209 | *Astral Projection* travels there with a silver cord (p. 111); excluded by *Forbiddance* (p. 133); random destination 91–00 on the *Amulet of the Planes* table (p. 209) |
| **Ethereal Plane / Border Ethereal** | SRD pp. 113, 128, 133, 160, 288, 311, 314 | *Etherealness*: "You step into the border regions of the Ethereal Plane, where it overlaps with your current plane" (p. 128); Outer Planes do **not** border it (p. 128); *Blink* shunts you there on a 4–6 (p. 113); *Leomund's Secret Chest* hides a chest there (p. 160); Ghost and Phase Spider traits reference it (pp. 288, 311, 314) |
| **Elemental Planes** | SRD pp. 118, 153, 209, 237, 254, 280, 283 | *Conjure Elemental* conjures "spirits from the Elemental Planes" (p. 118); *Plane Shift* example destination: "a specific city on the Elemental Plane of Fire" (p. 153); Elementals "are beings from the Elemental Planes" (p. 254); named as Plane of Air/Earth/Fire/Water (p. 209) |
| **Upper Planes** | SRD pp. 118, 254 | *Conjure Celestial* conjures "a spirit from the Upper Planes" (p. 118); Celestials are tied to them (p. 254) |
| **Lower Planes** | SRD pp. 76, 254 | The Fiend Patron section offers "Make a Deal with the Lower Planes" (p. 76); Fiends "are creatures tied to terrifying Lower Planes" (p. 254) |
| **Outer Planes** | SRD pp. 37, 54, 128, 180, 209, 215 | Channel Divinity draws energy "directly from the Outer Planes" for Clerics (p. 37) and Paladins (p. 54); spirits of the dead depart there (p. 180); two random tables list sixteen of them (pp. 209, 215) |
| **Far Realm** | SRD pp. 196, 258 | Named twice, never defined: a trap can fling a character "into the Far Realm" for a turn (p. 196, Sample Mental Stress Effects); an aboleth "revives with all its Hit Points in the Far Realm or another location chosen by the GM" (p. 258) |

> [!src] SRD p. 254 — Stat Block Overview
> "Elementals are beings from the Elemental Planes, such as efreet and water elementals. Fey are creatures tied to the Feywild or the forces of nature, such as dryads and goblins. Fiends are creatures tied to terrifying Lower Planes, such as balors and hell hounds. Giants are towering beings with humanlike shapes…"

> [!src] SRD p. 209 — Amulet of the Planes
> The 1d100 destination table: 01–60 the plane you named; 61–70 a random Inner Plane (1d6: Air, Earth, Fire, Water, Feywild, Shadowfell); 71–80 a "good" Outer Plane (1d8 above); 81–90 an "evil" Outer Plane (1d8 above); 91–00 the Astral Plane. Attunement requires a DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) check to name a destination deliberately.

> [!src] SRD p. 153 — Plane Shift
> "…such as a specific city on the Elemental Plane of Fire or palace on the second level of the Nine Hells." The SRD's only structural hint that the Nine Hells are layered.

## Planes are enforced by stat blocks

The fastest way to make planar cosmology matter at the table is these traits — they are not flavor, they are resurrection rules.

| Trait | Effect | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Exalted Restoration* | Die outside the plane → body vanishes, revive with full HP somewhere in **Mount Celestia** | Deva (SRD p. 279), Planetar (SRD p. 315), Solar (SRD p. 326) |
| *Diabolical Restoration* | Die outside the plane → body vanishes in sulfurous smoke, revive with full HP in the **Nine Hells** | Barbed Devil (SRD p. 262), Bone Devil (p. 267), Horned Devil (p. 273), Erinyes (p. 283), Ice Devil (p. 300), Pit Fiend (p. 315) |
| *Hellish Restoration* | Same idea for lemures | Lemure (SRD p. 304) |
| *Demonic Restoration* | Body dissolves into ichor, revive with full HP somewhere in the **Abyss** | Glabrezu (SRD p. 289), Hezrou (p. 298), Marilith (p. 306), Nalfeshnee (p. 310), Vrock (p. 336), Balor (p. 261) |
| *Eldritch Restoration* | Aboleth revives in the **Far Realm** or another GM-chosen location | Aboleth (SRD p. 258) |

**Worldbuilding consequence:** if your campaign plane-hops, killing a devil, demon, or celestial off-plane is a *temporary* solution unless the party can reach its home plane. That is a built-in adventure hook, straight from the stat blocks.

### Planar magic items

| Item | Page | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Amulet of the Planes* | SRD p. 209 | Deliberate *Plane Shift* on DC 15 Int (Arcana); misfire = the random 1d100 table |
| *Candle of Invocation* | SRD pp. 214–215 | 4-hour candle; can cast *Gate* (destroys it), keyed to a weighted 1d100 Outer Plane table |
| *Cubic Gate* | SRD p. 216 | Six faces keyed to six planes, **one is the Material Plane**; *Gate* or *Plane Shift* per face |
| *Well of Many Worlds* | SRD p. 253 | Unfold it → "a portal to another world or plane of existence"; GM decides where it leads |
| *Bag of Tricks* (all variants) | SRD pp. 212–213 | Daily: spits out its contents "onto another plane of existence" — GM chooses plane and time |
| *Portable Hole* | SRD p. 244 | Misfire: "a spatial rift sends the sphere and each creature… to a random plane of existence" |
| *Staff of the Magi* (explosion) | SRD pp. 246–247 | 50% chance to travel to a random plane instead of taking 16 × charges Force damage |

> [!tip] Turning cosmology into play
> Every planar travel spell and item above gives the GM a *decision point* rather than a fact: the destination is "determined by the GM" or "in general terms." Use those as your session hooks — the players have the keys, you decide which door they opened.

## Confirmed [GAP]s in this area

1. **No cosmology chapter, no plane diagram, no "transitive planes" term** — searched: `Transitive`, `Prime Material`, `Shadow Plane` → 0 hits.
2. **No rules for planar travel by mundane means** — no *portal*, *gate*, or *planescape*-style travel procedure; only spells and items.
3. **No planar influence on the Material Plane** — no corruption/ley-line/taint subsystem.
4. **No definitions for the Far Realm, Acheron's function, or layer structure** beyond the single "second level of the Nine Hells" phrase (SRD p. 153).
5. **No list of which Outer Planes are home to which creature types** — infer it from restoration traits only.
6. **The 5.1 planes appendix is gone.** The [official SRD FAQ](https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document) confirms 5.2 removed *The Planes of Existence* appendix; a free copy of that older text is linked in [[Free Resources Directory#Filling gaps from free, older, or open sources]].
