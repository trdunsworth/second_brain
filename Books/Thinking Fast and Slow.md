# Thinking, Fast and Slow

## Summary

Kahneman's foundational work on the two systems that drive how we think — System 1 (fast, intuitive) and System 2 (slow, deliberate).

## Key Takeaways

1. Cognitive biases aren't bugs — they're features that misfire
2. Anchoring effects are more powerful than we realize
3. Overconfidence is the most persistent bias

## How I'll Use This

Applicable to forecasting model evaluation — watch for anchoring in expert judgment.
