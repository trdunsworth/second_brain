## 6. Study Strategy for Specialized Vocabulary

### Per Domain
1. **Core 100 terms** - High-frequency, must-know
2. **Collocations** - Verb + Noun pairs (診断をつける, 契約を締結する)
3. **Document templates** - Standard phrases for emails, reports
4. **Bilingual glossary** - Build your own

### Resources
- **JLPT N1/N2 specialized decks** (Anki)
- **業界用語辞典** (Industry dictionaries)
- **専門書の索引** (Textbook indices)
- **業界ニュース** (Nikkei, ITmedia, Medical Tribune)
- **専門家のツイート/ブログ** (Twitter, note, Qiita)

### Learning Order
1. **Your current field** (work/study)
2. **Adjacent fields** (collaboration partners)
3. **High-interest fields** (curiosity)
4. **General business** (universal)

---

*Specialized vocabulary = professional credibility. You don't need all domains. Master YOUR field's 500 core terms + collocations. The rest is exposure.*
