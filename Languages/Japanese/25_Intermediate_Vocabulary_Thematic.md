# Intermediate Vocabulary - Thematic Word Lists (N3-N2 Level)

## Study Strategy
- Learn in **context** (sentences, not isolation)
- Group by **theme** for associative memory
- Use **Anki** with cloze deletion or sentence cards
- Target: **2,000-3,000 words** for N3→N2

## Study Tips for Intermediate Vocab

1. **Learn Kanji + Word Together** - Don't separate
2. **Collocations First** - 会議を**する**, 報告を**する**, not just nouns
3. **Register Awareness** - 朝食 vs 朝ごはん, 報告する vs 言う
4. **Thematic Anki Decks** - One deck per theme above
5. **Input Flood** - Read news, watch dramas in each theme
6. **Output Practice** - Write 3 sentences per new word

---

*Vocabulary is infinite. These 10 themes cover 80% of daily adult conversation. Master them, then specialize to your interests (IT, finance, literature, etc.).*
