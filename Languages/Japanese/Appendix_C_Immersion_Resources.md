# Immersion Resources (インプット・リソース) - Massive Input Library

## Philosophy: Input Hypothesis
**Comprehensible input** drives acquisition. Target **i+1** (slightly above current level). Massive volume > perfect understanding. **Enjoyment** sustains consistency.

## Resource Selection Strategy

### By Level
| Level | Primary | Secondary | Tertiary |
|-------|---------|-----------|----------|
| **N5-N4** | Graded readers, Tadoku, NHK Easy, Yotsuba&!, Satori Reader | Genki/Minna no Nihongo, Cure Dolly, Comprehensible JP | Anime: Polar Bear Cafe, Doraemon |
| **N3** | Graded readers Lv 2-3, Manga (Yotsuba, Slam Dunk), Satori Reader, NHK Easy | Tobira, Japanese Ammo, Nihongo con Teppei | Anime: Non Non Biyori, Spy×Family |
| **N2** | Manga (Shonen Jump+), Native novels (Kadokawa Tsubasa), News (NHK), Podcasts (Noriko, Shun) | Tobira advanced, Dogen (pitch), Native anime/drama | Variety shows, News Web |
| **N1** | Native everything: novels (Shincho/Iwanami), News (Nikkei/Asahi), Variety, Podcasts (COTEN, Bilingual) | Specialized: Academic papers, Legal docs, Business books | Translation practice, Creative writing |

### By Goal
| Goal | Resources |
|------|-----------|
| **JLPT N2** | Tango N2, Sou Matome N2, NHK News, Shonen Jump manga, Noriko/Shun podcasts |
| **Business Japanese** | NewsPicks, Nikkei, Business YouTubers, Keigo drills, Email templates |
| **Literature** | Kadokawa Tsubasa → Shincho Bunko → Iwanami Bunko, Literary podcasts, BookTubers |
| **Daily Life** | NHK Easy, Yotsuba&!, Satori Reader, Variety shows, Dramas (Nigeru wa Haji) |
| **Pitch Accent** | Dogen, OJAD, NHK Accent Dictionary, Forvo, Native audio + mimicry |

## Immersion Schedule Template (Daily 1-2 Hours)

| Time | Activity | Resource Type | Duration |
|------|----------|---------------|----------|
| **Morning** | Anki Reviews | SRS | 15-20 min |
| **Commute** | Podcast / Audiobook | Audio | 30 min |
| **Lunch** | Reading (Manga/News/Novel) | Visual + Text | 20 min |
| **Evening** | Anime/Drama/Variety | Audio-Visual | 30-60 min |
| **Before Bed** | Light Reading / Review | Text | 15 min |

### Weekend Deep Dive
- **Saturday**: 1-2 hour native content (movie, long drama, novel chapter)
- **Sunday**: Rest or light review / writing practice / speaking practice

| Metric | Tool | Target |
|--------|------|--------|
| **Reading (chars/day)** | Mokuro/Kindle/Manual | 5,000-10,000 |
| **Listening (hours/week)** | Podcast app/Manual | 5-10 hrs |
| **Anki Reviews/day** | Anki Stats | 100-200 |
| **New Words/week** | Mining log / Anki | 50-100 |
| **Enjoyment (1-10)** | Self-rate daily | 7+ |

---

*Immersion is not "study" - it's **life in Japanese**. The best resource is the one you **actually consume**. Curate a feed you **look forward to**. Consistency > perfection. 1 hour daily > 10 hours weekly.*
