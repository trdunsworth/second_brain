# Translation Notes (翻訳メモ - 日英・英日)

## Overview
Translation is **not word substitution** - it's **meaning transfer** across different linguistic/cultural systems. These notes highlight systematic mismatches.

---

## Structural Mismatches

### 1. Word Order (SOV vs SVO)
| Japanese | English | Strategy |
|----------|---------|----------|
| **私は昨日、図書館で本を読みました。** | I read a book at the library yesterday. | **Reorder**: Time/Place before verb |
| **彼が来ることを知っています。** | I know that he will come. | **Clause embedding**: こと → that |
| **雨が降ったので、行きません。** | Since it rained, I won't go. | **Front reason clause** |

### 2. Topic vs Subject (は vs が)
| Japanese | English | Note |
|----------|---------|------|
| **象は鼻が長い。** | Elephants have long trunks. | **Topic-comment** → Subject-verb |
| **私が行く。** | I'll go. | **Focus** (が) → Emphasis/stress |
| **雨が降っている。** | It's raining. | **Phenomenon subject** → Dummy "it" |

### 3. Null Arguments (Zero Pronouns)
| Japanese | English | Strategy |
|----------|---------|----------|
| **食べた。** | I ate (it). | **Restore** from context |
| **行きますか。行く。** | Are you going? Yes, I am. | **Supply** subject/verb |
| **知らない。** | I don't know. | **Supply** "I" |

## Vocabulary Mismatches

### 1. Verbs of Giving/Receiving (授受動詞)
| Japanese | English | Context |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **あげる** | give (outward) | **Direction** matters |
| **くれる** | give (inward) | **Benefactive** nuance |
| **もらう** | receive / have done | **Receiver** as subject |
| **いただく** | receive (humble) | **Register** |
| **くださる** | give (honorific) | **Register** |
| **差し上げる** | give (humble) | **Register** |

> **Translation**: Often requires restructuring
> - **先生が本をくださった。** → The teacher **gave me** a book. (NOT "The teacher gave-down a book")

### 2. Stative Verbs as Adjectives
| Japanese | English | Grammar |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **好きだ** | like | **Adjective** in English |
| **嫌いだ** | dislike | **Adjective** |
| **欲しい** | want | **Adjective** (transitive in JP) |
| **分かる** | understand | **Verb** (but stative) |
| **できる** | can | **Modal verb** |
| **要る** | need | **Verb** (stative) |

> **Particle shift**: が → **object** in English
> - **日本語が分かる。** → I understand Japanese. (NOT "Japanese understands")

### 3. Aspect vs Tense
| Japanese | English | Mapping |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **食べた** | ate / have eaten | **Perfective** → Past / Present Perfect |
| **食べている** | am eating / have been eating | **Progressive** / **Perfect** |
| **食べていた** | was eating / had been eating | **Past Progressive** / **Past Perfect** |
| **食べたことがある** | have eaten before | **Experiential** → Present Perfect |

### 4. Modality (Modal Verbs)
| Japanese | English | Nuance |
|----------|---------|--------|
| **なければならない** | must / have to | **External obligation** |
| **べきだ** | should / ought to | **Moral/logical** |
| **ことができる** | can / be able to | **Ability** |
| **可能性がある** | may / might | **Possibility** |
| **だろう / でしょう** | probably / will | **Conjecture** |
| **かもしれない** | may / might | **Weak possibility** |
| **はずだ** | should / supposed to | **Expectation** |
| **にちがいない** | must be / no doubt | **Certainty** |

## Cultural/Conceptual Mismatches

### 1. Honorifics (敬語) - Untranslatable Directly
| Japanese | English Strategy |
|----------|------------------|
| **尊敬語** (Sonkeigo) | **Formal register**, passive, titles (Mr./Prof.) |
| **謙譲語** (Kenjougo) | **Self-deprecation**, "I was allowed to..." |
| **丁寧語** (Teineigo) | **Polite register** (please, could you) |
| **美化語** (Bikago) | **Euphemism**, "restroom" not "toilet" |

> **Example**: **ご覧になりましたか。** → **Did you see it?** (Formal) / **Have you had a chance to look at it?**

### 2. 申し訳ありません / 恐縮です - Apology Nuances
| Japanese | English | Context |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **申し訳ありません** | I apologize / I'm sorry | Standard formal |
| **恐縮です** | I'm terribly sorry / I feel bad | **Receiving favor** |
| **恐れ入ります** | I'm obliged / Thank you | **Receiving kindness** |
| **お手数をおかけします** | Thank you for your trouble | **Requesting effort** |
| **ご迷惑をおかけします** | I apologize for the inconvenience | **Causing trouble** |

### 3. よろしくお願いします - The Universal Phrase
| Context | English Equivalent |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Meeting start** | Nice to meet you / Looking forward to working with you |
| **Request** | Please / Thank you in advance |
| **Email close** | Best regards / Thank you |
| **New Year** | Happy New Year |
| **After favor** | Thank you / I appreciate it |

> **No single English equivalent** - translate by **function**.

### 4. お疲れ様 / ご苦労様 - Workplace Acknowledgments
| Phrase | Direction | English |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| **お疲れ様です** | Peer → Peer / Subordinate → Superior | Good work / Thanks for your work |
| **お疲れ様でした** | End of day / After task | Good job today / Thanks for your hard work |
| **ご苦労様** | Superior → Subordinate | Good work (downward only) |
| **ご苦労様です** | Superior → Subordinate (ongoing) | Keep up the good work |

### 5. いただきます / ご馳走様 - Meal Rituals
| Phrase | Timing | English |
|--------|--------|---------|
| **いただきます** | Before eating | Let's eat / Thanks for the food |
| **ご馳走様でした** | After eating | That was delicious / Thank you for the meal |

## Genre-Specific Translation

### 1. Business Email
| Japanese Pattern | English Template |
|------------------|------------------|
| **お世話になっております。** | Thank you for your continued support. / I hope this email finds you well. |
| **ご検討のほど、よろしくお願いいたします。** | We would appreciate your consideration. / We look forward to your feedback. |
| **ご査収ください。** | Please find attached. / Please review the attached. |
| **ご教示いただけますと幸いです。** | We would appreciate your guidance. / Please let us know. |

### 2. Academic Paper
| Japanese | English |
|----------|---------|
| **本研究では、〜を目的とする。** | **This study aims to...** / **The purpose of this study is to...** |
| **〜と考えられる。** | **It is suggested that...** / **It is considered that...** |
| **〜が示唆された。** | **...was suggested.** / **...was implied.** |
| **以上のことから、〜と結論づけられる。** | **From the above, it can be concluded that...** |
| **今後の課題である。** | **This remains a topic for future research.** |

### 3. Literature/Fiction
| Japanese Feature | English Strategy |
|------------------|------------------|
| **体言止め** (Taigen-dome) | **Sentence fragments** / Noun phrases |
| **文語混在** | **Elevated register** / Archaic touches |
| **擬音語・擬態語** | **Sound words** / **Descriptive verbs** |
| **省略** | **Ellipsis** / **Implication** |
| **季語** (Kigo) | **Seasonal reference** (often keep or footnote) |

> **Example**: **雨。冷たい風。誰もいない駅。**
> **Translation**: Rain. Cold wind. An empty station.

### 4. Manga/Anime Subtitles
| Constraint | Strategy |
|------------|----------|
| **Character limit** (2 lines, ~32 chars/line) | **Condense**, keep **core meaning** |
| **Reading speed** (12-15 cps) | **Simplify syntax**, active verbs |
| **Tone/personality** | **Register matching** (rude, cute, formal) |
| **Culture-specific** | **Localize** or **retain + note** |

## Common Translation Errors

### 1. Literal Particle Translation
| Error | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| **彼は学生です。** → "He **is** a student." | ✅ (OK) |
| **本が好きです。** → "Book **ga** like." | ❌ → "I **like** books." |
| **雨が降っています。** → "Rain **ga** falls." | ❌ → "It **is raining**." |

### 2. Tense/Aspect Mismatch
| Error | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| **日本に行ったことがあります。** → "I went to Japan." | ❌ → "I **have been to** Japan." |
| **今、食べています。** → "I eat now." | ❌ → "I **am eating**." |

### 3. Honorific Over/Under-translation
| Error | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| **ご覧になりましたか。** → "Did you look at it honorifically?" | ❌ → "**Did you see it?**" (Formal) |
| **申し訳ありません。** → "It cannot be excused." | ❌ → "**I apologize.**" / "**I'm sorry.**" |

### 4. Stative Verb Object
| Error | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| **日本語が分かります。** → "Japanese understands." | ❌ → "**I understand Japanese.**" |
| **車が欲しいです。** → "Car wants." | ❌ → "**I want a car.**" |

### 5. Giving/Receiving Direction
| Error | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| **先生が本をくれた。** → "Teacher gave book." | ❌ → "**Teacher gave ME a book.**" |
| **先生に本をあげた。** → "Gave to teacher." | ✅ (But: "I gave the teacher a book") |

## Untranslatable Concepts (Culture-Bound)

| Concept | Japanese | Explanation | Translation Strategy |
|---------|----------|-------------|---------------------|
| **侘び寂び** | わびさび | Beauty in imperfection/impermanence | **Wabi-sabi** (retain + gloss) |
| **物の哀れ** | もののあわれ | Pathos of transience | **Mono no aware** (retain) |
| **間** | ま | Negative space; timing; pause | **Ma** (retain) / "Pause" / "Interval" |
| **以心伝心** | いしんでんしん | Heart-to-heart understanding | **Telepathy** / "Unspoken understanding" |
| **おもてなし** | おもてなし | Wholehearted hospitality | **Omotenashi** / "Hospitality" |
| **義理** | ぎり | Social obligation/duty | **Giri** / "Obligation" |
| **人情** | にんじょう | Human feeling/compassion | **Ninjō** / "Humanity" |
| **恩** | おん | Debt of gratitude | **On** / "Debt of gratitude" |
| **面目** | めんぼく | Face; reputation | **Face** / "Reputation" |
| **建前vs本音** | たてまえvsほんね | Public stance vs true feelings | **Tatemae/honne** (retain) |

## Translation Workflow

### 1. Analysis (解析)
- Identify **genre, audience, purpose**
- Segment into **meaning units** (not sentences)
- Flag **culture-bound terms**, **ambiguities**

### 2. Draft (下訳)
- **Meaning-first**, not word-for-word
- **Natural target syntax** from start
- **Mark uncertainties** [?]

### 3. Revision (推敲)
- **Read aloud** in target language
- Check **register consistency**
- **Verify terminology** (glossaries)
- **Native review** if possible

### 4. QA (品質確認)
- **Numbers, dates, names** - exact
- **Formatting, tags, variables** - intact
- **Character limits** (UI/subtitles) - met
- **Terminology consistency** - verified
