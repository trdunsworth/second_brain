# Giving & Receiving (授受動詞 - じゅじゅどうし) - Complete System

## The Three Core Verbs

| Verb | Reading | Direction | Politeness | Use For |
|------|---------|-----------|------------|---------|
| **あげる** (ageru) | あげる | **Outward** (Me → Other / Other → Other) | Neutral | General giving |
| **くれる** (kureru) | くれる | **Inward** (Other → Me / Other → My in-group) | Neutral | Receiving (favor) |
| **もらう** (morau) | もらう | **Inward** (Me ← Other / Other ← Other) | Neutral | Receiving (action focus) |

> **Key**: Direction is relative to **speaker's in-group (ウチ - uchi)**!

---

## Directionality Map

```
                    OUTWARD (あげる)
        Me/My Group ─────────────► Others
              ▲                   │
              │                   │
              │  INWARD (くれる)   │
              │                   ▼
        Others ────────────────► Me/My Group
                    INWARD (もらう)
```

### あげる (Outward)
- **私は田中さんに本をあげた。** (I gave Tanaka a book.)
- **田中さんは山田さんに本をあげた。** (Tanaka gave Yamada a book.)
- **Focus**: Giver's perspective, outward flow.

### くれる (Inward - Favor)
- **田中さんが私に本をくれた。** (Tanaka gave me a book.)
- **田中さんが私の妹に本をくれた。** (Tanaka gave my sister a book.)
- **Focus**: Receiver's perspective, gratitude, "did me a favor."

### もらう (Inward - Receive/Action)
- **私は田中さんに本をもらった。** (I received a book from Tanaka.)
- **田中さんは山田さんに本をもらった。** (Tanaka received a book from Yamada.)
- **Focus**: Receiver's action, "I got X from Y."

---

## Particle Patterns (Critical!)

### あげる / くれる: Giver = Subject (が/は), Receiver = に, Object = を
> **Giver** が/は **Receiver** に **Object** を あげる/くれる

| Role | Particle | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| **Giver** | が / は | 私が / 田中さんが |
| **Receiver** | **に** | 田中さん**に** / 私**に** |
| **Object** | を | 本**を** |

### もらう: Receiver = Subject (が/は), Giver = に/から, Object = を
> **Receiver** が/は **Giver** に/から **Object** を もらう

| Role | Particle | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| **Receiver** | が / は | 私が / 田中さんが |
| **Giver** | **に / から** | 田中さん**に** / 田中さん**から** |
| **Object** | を | 本**を** |

> **から** emphasizes source/origin; **に** is standard.

## Politeness Hierarchy (Keigo Versions)

| Base | Humble (謙譲語 - Kenjougo) | Honorific (尊敬語 - Sonkeigo) |
|------|---------------------------|------------------------------|
| **あげる** | **差し上げる** (sashiageru) | **お与えになる** (o-atae ni naru) |
| **くれる** | — | **くださる** (kudasaru) |
| **もらう** | **いただく** (itadaku) | **お受けになる** (o-uke ni naru) |

### Usage Rules
- **Humble (Kenjougo)**: My/In-group's action → Outsider
  - **先生に本を差し上げた。** (Gave book to teacher - humble)
  - **先生から本をいただいた。** (Received book from teacher - humble)

- **Honorific (Sonkeigo)**: Outsider's action → Me/In-group
  - **先生が本をくださった。** (Teacher gave me book - honorific)
  - **先生が本をお受けになった。** (Teacher received book - honorific)

- **Neutral**: In-group ↔ In-group, or casual
  - **友達に本をあげた。** (Gave friend book.)
  - **友達から本をもらった。** (Got book from friend.)

## Giving/Receiving Actions (Verbs in て-form)

### Structure: [Verb て-form] + あげる/くれる/もらう

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **〜てあげる** | Do something **for someone** (outward) | **荷物を持ってあげた。** (Carried luggage for them.) |
| **〜てくれる** | Someone **does for me/us** (inward, gratitude) | **田中さんが荷物を持ってくれた。** (Tanaka carried luggage for me.) |
| **〜てもらう** | **Have someone do** for me (inward, action focus) | **田中さんに荷物を持ってもらった。** (Had Tanaka carry luggage.) |

### Particle Rules with Verbs
| Pattern | Doer (Subject) | Receiver (Target) | Object/Action |
|---------|----------------|-------------------|---------------|
| **〜てあげる** | Me/In-group | **に** (Person) | Verbて |
| **〜てくれる** | Other | Me/In-group (implied) | Verbて |
| **〜てもらう** | Me/In-group | **に/から** (Doer) | Verbて |

### Examples
> **私は弟に宿題を教えてあげた。** (I taught HW to brother.)
> **弟が私に宿題を教えてくれた。** (Brother taught me HW - thanks!)
> **私は弟に宿題を教えてもらった。** (I had brother teach me HW.)

---

## Keigo with Action Verbs

| Base | Humble (My action for superior) | Honorific (Superior's action for me) |
|------|---------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| **〜てあげる** | **〜て差し上げる** | **〜てくださる** |
| **〜てもらう** | **〜ていただく** | — |

### Examples
> **先生に質問して差し上げた。** (Asked teacher a question - humble)
> **先生が質問してくださった。** (Teacher asked me - honorific)
> **先生に質問していただいた。** (Had teacher ask me / Teacher asked me - humble)
> **先生に手紙を書いていただいた。** (Had teacher write letter / Teacher wrote letter for me.)

---

## Special: よこす (yokosu) - "Give Me" (Casual/Male)

- **俺にくれよ！** → **俺によこせ！** (Give it to me!)
- **Direction**: Other → Me (like くれる)
- **Register**: Rough, masculine, casual. **Never use with superiors!**
- **Imperative**: よこせ

---

## Special: やる (yaru) - "Give" (Downward/Animals/Plants)

| Target | Verb | Example |
|--------|------|---------|
| **Animals/Plants** | **やる** | **猫に餌をやった。** (Fed the cat.) |
| **Children/Inferior (Casual)** | **やる** | **弟にお小遣いをやった。** (Gave brother allowance.) |
| **Equals/Superiors** | **あげる** | **先生に本をあげた。** (Gave teacher book.) |

> **やる** = Downward only. **あげる** = Neutral/Upward.

## Summary Decision Tree

```
Who is the GIVER?
├── Me / My In-group (ウチ)
│   └── Who is the RECEIVER?
│       ├── Other / Superior → **差し上げる** (Humble)
│       ├── Equal / Friend → **あげる** (Neutral)
│       ├── Child / Pet / Inferior → **やる** (Downward)
│       └── Action: Do for them → **〜てあげる** / **〜て差し上げる**
│
├── Other / Superior (ソト)
│   └── Who is the RECEIVER?
│       ├── Me / My In-group → **くださる** (Honorific) / **くれる** (Neutral)
│       ├── Other / Third Party → **あげる** (Neutral observation)
│       └── Action: Do for me → **〜てくれる** / **〜てくださる**
│
Who is the RECEIVER? (Focus on receiving)
├── Me / My In-group
│   └── Who is the GIVER?
│       ├── Superior → **いただく** (Humble)
│       ├── Other → **もらう** (Neutral)
│       └── Action: Have them do → **〜てもらう** / **〜ていただく**
│
└── Other
    └── Who is the GIVER?
        ├── Me → **あげる** / **差し上げる**
        └── Third Party → **もらう** (Observation: "Tanaka got it from Yamada")
```

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correct | Reason |
|---------|---------|--------|
| 先生に本を**あげた** | 先生に本を**差し上げた** | Superior → Humble |
| 先生が本を**あげた** | 先生が本を**くださった** | Superior to me → Honorific |
| 友達に本を**くれた** | 友達に本を**あげた** | Me → Friend = outward |
| 先生から本を**もらった** | 先生から本を**いただいた** | Superior → Humble |
| 猫に餌を**あげた** | 猫に餌を**やった** | Animal = やる |
| 弟に宿題を**やってあげた** | 弟に宿題を**やってあげた** | (Actually OK - やる for action, あげる for giving act) |

## Practice: Choose the Verb

1. I gave my teacher a gift. → **先生にプレゼントを差し上げた。**
2. My teacher gave me a gift. → **先生がプレゼントをくださった。**
3. I received a gift from my teacher. → **先生からプレゼントをいただいた。**
4. I gave my friend a book. → **友達に本をあげた。**
5. My friend gave me a book. → **友達が本をくれた。**
6. I got a book from my friend. → **友達から本をもらった。**
7. I taught Japanese to my boss. → **部長に日本語を教えて差し上げた。**
8. My boss taught me Japanese. → **部長が日本語を教えてくださった。**
9. I had my boss check my email. → **部長にメールをチェックしていただいた。**
10. I fed the cat. → **猫に餌をやった。**

---

*The giving/receiving system encodes social hierarchy. Every exchange positions you relative to others. Master the directionality (ウチ→ソト, ソト→ウチ) and the keigo triplet (あげる/差し上げる, くれる/くださる, もらう/いただく).*
