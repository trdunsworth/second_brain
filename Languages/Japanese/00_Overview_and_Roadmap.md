# Japanese Language Learning Roadmap

This collection of notes is designed to take you from **basic fundamentals** through **literary/advanced Japanese**, with the goal of regaining conversational fluency and continuing to improve beyond.

## Study Principles
1. **Input before output** - Massive comprehensible input drives acquisition
2. **Kanji in context** - Learn kanji through vocabulary, not isolation
3. **Grammar as patterns** - Internalize through exposure, not just rule memorization
4. **Pitch accent awareness** - Develop from day one, refine continuously
5. **Active recall > passive review** - Use SRS (Anki) effectively
6. **Balanced skills** - Listening, reading, speaking, writing in rotation
