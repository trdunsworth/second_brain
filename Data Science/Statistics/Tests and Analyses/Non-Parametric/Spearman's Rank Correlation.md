# Spearman's Rank Correlation ($\rho$)

Spearman's correlation is the non-parametric version of the Pearson Correlation Coefficient.

## 1. When to Use
- **Goal**: Measure the strength and direction of the **monotonic** relationship between two variables.
- **Data Type**: Ordinal data or non-linear (but monotonic) continuous data.
- **Monotonic**: A relationship where as one variable increases, the other *either* increases or decreases, but not necessarily in a straight line.

## 2. Key Difference from Pearson
- **Pearson**: Measures **linear** relationships (straight line).
- **Spearman**: Measures **monotonic** relationships (any consistent direction). It uses the ranks of the values rather than the values themselves.

## 3. Interpretation
- **+1.0**: Perfect positive monotonic relationship.
- **0.0**: No monotonic relationship.
- **-1.0**: Perfect negative monotonic relationship.
