## SQL Statement

```sql
USE Reporting_System;
GO

SELECT rmi.Master_Incident_Number,
	rmi.Response_Date,
	rmi.Problem,
	rmi.Agency_Type,
	rc.Comment
FROM Response_Master_Incident rmi
	JOIN Response_Comments rc ON rmi.ID = rc.Master_Incident_ID
WHERE rmi.Response_Date > '2026-09-01'
AND ((rc.Comment LIKE '%DRONE%') OR (rc.Comment LIKE '%UAS5'));
```
