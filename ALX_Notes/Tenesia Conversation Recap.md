
## Thoughts About an Analytical Agent in DECC

Following up from the conversation from [[2026-10-07]], here is the layout that I envision. 

### DEC Data Professional/Manager

First, I'm changing the name because the incumbent may be doing more than just analytics. This role would likely take care of reporting, most ad hoc data requests, analytics, and data-centric business analysis. It may also take on the role of a secondary technical administrator with respect to software like Moodle or GovWorx.

The position would interact most with the watch officers, training officers, QA/QI officers, and the accreditation manager to facilitate the use of the centre's data to improve performance of the centre, as well as conditions in the centre.

I can see the data officer working with the watch officers to evaluate KPIs and policies to see their impact on operations and to determine how other factors play into operations. They are, also, the normal subject of the weekly and monthly report cycles. 

The data officer can also work with the training manager to evaluate things like how effective supplemental training or continuing education is with respect to policy and desired outcomes. A rolling experiment cycle of monthly training could have efficacy checks using data from the month prior to training, weekly checks the week of training, and the month after training with follow up to see if additional training is working. Survival analyses can be conducted during training sessions to determine when most candidates are dropping out and see if we can identify when, where, and why we are losing candidates prior to graduation to service. 

I think we could deploy [Moodle](https://moodle.org/) or [Blackboard](https://www.blackboard.com/) as an LMS to develop both the academy courses and the continuing education classes. This would allow for telecommunicators to take the continuing education courses on their own time. We could also track the training results and any testing that you would want to create. 

The data officer could also work with the QA team to help identify trends for analysis and see how education and remediation actions have taken effect. All of these become self-feeding cycles that allow us to work on a principle of continuous improvement.
